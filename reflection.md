In this assignment, I feel like I finally got a more intuitive understanding of how inheritance actually works in practice. Instead of rewriting everything from scratch, I could rely on the Building class and just build on top of it. It made the structure of the code feel much cleaner and more logical, and I didn’t have to repeat things unnecessarily.

What worked well for me was going step by step and really following the structure given. Once I figured out how the parent class connects to the subclasses, the rest started to make more sense. Using ArrayList also helped a lot, since they handled most of the data management for me.

One thing that was a bit tricky was keeping track of all the small requirements, especially making sure every method matched the interface exactly. It’s easy to miss small details like return types or method names. I also had to slow down and be more careful with formatting and comments than I usually am.

If I were to give advice, I would say don’t rush into coding right away. Spend a little time understanding how everything is connected first, and then implement things one by one. Also, checking the rubric early and often really helps, because it’s easy to lose points on small things.
